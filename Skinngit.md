> **instrait** — Lock your commitment. Pledge your capital. Execute without an escape route.

## What Is instrait?

instrait is execution infrastructure built around one idea: **you follow through when you have no exit route.**

The human brain is hardwired to seek the path of least resistance. When a system allows you to negotiate, you will negotiate. When it allows extensions, you will take them. instrait removes those options entirely — it is a deliberate narrowing of freedom that forces velocity.

A **strait** in geography is a narrow, compressed passage connecting two bodies of water. It does not create new water — it forces existing volume through a restricted space, exponentially increasing velocity. instrait applies this principle to execution: shrink the freedom, scale the output.

**Shrink the freedom. Scale the output.**

## The Main Sections

### Home — Your Daily Command Center

The first thing you see every day is your **Home screen** — a focused dashboard showing exactly what must be executed today.

- **Today's tasks** — all the steps from your goals that are due today, laid out in a simple timeline
- **Today's habits** — your recurring commitments with a one-tap check-off
- **Upcoming tasks** — a preview of what's coming in the next few days
- **Current streak** — a visible counter showing how many days in a row you've executed
- **Week strip** — tap any day in the past week to review your record

---

### Goals — Define the Node

Goals are the primary node in instrait. A node is anything you want to achieve with a strict deadline — a project, a skill, a habit system, a milestone.

**When you create a goal you can:**

- Choose a **category** (Health & Fitness, Career, Finance, Skills, Relationships, Mindset, and more)
- Set a **start date** and a **strict deadline**
- Select your **experience level** (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) so the plan fits your reality
- Let the **AI generate a full plan** — milestones and tasks are automatically created based on your goal description
- Or build the plan **manually** by adding your own milestones and tasks

**Inside each goal you'll find:**

- A **progress bar** showing how far along you are
- A list of **milestones** (major checkpoints) and **tasks** (individual action items)
- The ability to **mark tasks complete** right from the goal detail screen
- A **pledge** — an optional financial commitment that anchors your goal to real capital (see Wallet section)

**Goal categories available:**

Health & Fitness · Career & Work · Finance & Money · Skills & Learning · Relationships · Mindset & Wellbeing · Leisure & Adventure · Home & Environment · Giving & Contribution · Personal · Other

### Habits — Build Your Daily Routines

Habits are recurring commitments that compound into structural change. The Habits section is designed to make consistency non-negotiable.

**When you create a habit you can:**

- Give it a name, color, and icon
- Choose the **type**:
  - *Binary* — did you execute it or not? (e.g., "Meditate today")
  - *Quantitative* — track a number toward a target (e.g., "Drink 2 liters of water")
- Set the **frequency**: daily, every few days, weekly, only weekdays, only weekends, or specific days
- Set a **duration**: open-ended, with an end date, or with a target number of completions
- Optionally **pledge money** on it — if you miss an occurrence, a small charge is automatically applied to your saved card

**Inside each habit:**

- A **heatmap calendar** showing your execution history at a glance
- Your current **streak** and longest streak
- A breakdown of completion rate over time
- An option to **give up** the habit (with a calculated penalty if you have a pledge)

### Social Hub — Accountability With Real People

The Social section extends instrait beyond individual discipline. Here you connect with others and use **social accountability** as a second engine of execution.

#### Feed

- Browse public **commitments** made by other users in the community
- Filter to see only people **you follow**
- React, verify, or call out others' progress

#### Commitment Board

A commitment is a public declaration: *"I will do X by Y date."* You post it publicly so others can see and verify it.

- Create a commitment with a title, deadline, and optional **shame duration** (if you fail, a shame badge stays on your profile)
- Set visibility: **Private**, visible to **Followers**, or visible to **Accountability Partners**
- **Resolve** your own commitment as completed or failed when the time comes
- Other users can **verify** your completion or **call you out** if they think you failed

#### Partners — Your Accountability Circle

Partners are a higher level of connection than followers. A partner relationship is **mutual and approved by both sides**.

- Send a **partner request** to someone whose goals align with yours
- Once accepted, you appear in each other's partner list
- Partners can see each other's partner-visibility content
- Find potential partners through the **Matchmaking** board
- When a goal with a pledge is forfeited, the pledged amount can be distributed to your partners as a reward for holding you accountable

#### Following

- **Follow** any public user to see their activity in your feed
- View someone's public profile to see their active goals and public commitments
- Browse **Followers** and **Following** lists, plus a dedicated **Partners** tab

### Wallet — Financial Pledges & Payment Cards

The Wallet is where instrait makes commitment real. **Financial pledges** are the primary accountability mechanism — loss aversion is a hardwired biological force, and anchoring your goal to real capital changes the psychological game entirely.

#### How Pledges Work

When you create a goal or habit, you can attach a **financial pledge**:

1. Save a debit or credit card in the Wallet
2. Set a pledge amount on a specific goal or habit
3. If you **succeed** — your card is never charged
4. If you **fail** — the pledged amount is automatically charged to your saved card
5. The money goes to a **charity of your choice** (UNICEF, WHO, Red Cross, Doctors Without Borders, and others) or to your **accountability partners**

This is not a punishment — it is a **pre-commitment device**. You decide upfront what failure costs, sealing your future self into the agreement before weakness arrives.

> Procrastination is no longer free. It has a clear, non-negotiable price tag.

#### Payment Cards

- Add and manage your saved cards securely (powered by **Stripe**)
- Set a default card for automatic charges
- View which goals and habits currently have active pledges

#### Transaction History

- Full log of all charges and payments
- See exactly which goal or habit triggered each transaction

### Strait Score & Achievements — Your Execution Record

instrait tracks your record with a **Strait Score** — not a vanity metric, but a precise reflection of how reliably you follow through on your own word.

- **Strait Score** — earned by completing goals, keeping habits, and fulfilling commitments. It rises when you execute. It drops when you don't.
- **Achievements** — milestones unlocked through consistent execution
- **Redemption Mode** — if your Strait Score falls too low, you enter a 7-day recovery streak requirement before your standing is restored

There are no shortcuts. The Strait Score is a mirror.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my money safe?**
Yes. Card payments are handled by Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1). instrait never stores your card details — only a secure token.

**What happens if I fail a goal with a pledge?**
The pledged amount is charged to your saved card and sent to the charity you chose when setting up the pledge. You decide the destination before you start.

**Can I remove a pledge after I set it?**
Pledges are intentionally hard to remove — that friction is the feature. The commitment needs to feel real.

**Can I delete a habit?**
You can delete a habit only on the same day you created it. After that, it is a commitment you have made to yourself.

**Is the social feed anonymous?**
No — commitments and goals shared publicly are tied to your username. Accountability only works when it's real.

**What languages does the app support?**
The app interface is in English and Czech.
