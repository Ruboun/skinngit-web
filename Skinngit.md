# Skinngit — Your Personal Growth Companion

  

> **Welcome to Skinngit** — the app that turns your ambitions into real results by combining smart planning, daily discipline, and social accountability.

  

---

  

## What Is Skinngit?

  

Skinngit is a self-improvement app built around one idea: **you are more likely to achieve your goals when you have a clear plan, daily structure, and real consequences for giving up.**

  

Whether you want to get fit, learn a new skill, save money, or build a daily routine — Skinngit gives you the tools to make it happen. And if you need an extra push, you can put real money on the line or invite a friend to hold you accountable.

  

---

  

## The Main Sections

  

### Home — Your Daily Command Center

  

The first thing you see every day is your **Home screen** — a warm, friendly dashboard that shows you exactly what needs to be done today.

  

- **Today's tasks** — all the small steps from your goals that are due today, laid out in a simple timeline

- **Today's habits** — your recurring routines with a one-tap check-off button

- **Upcoming tasks** — a preview of what's coming in the next few days so nothing catches you off guard

- **Current streak** — a visible counter that shows how many days in a row you've been consistent

- **Week strip** — tap any day in the past week to quickly review what you did

  

---

  

### Goals — Turn Dreams Into Actionable Plans

  

Goals are the heart of Skinngit. A goal is anything meaningful you want to achieve — running a marathon, finishing an online course, saving up for a trip, building a side project.

  

**When you create a goal you can:**

- Choose a **category** (Health & Fitness, Career, Finance, Skills, Relationships, Mindset, and more)

- Set a **start date** and a **deadline**

- Select your **experience level** (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) so the plan fits your reality

- Let the **AI generate a full plan** — milestones and tasks are automatically created for you based on your goal description

- Or build the plan **manually** by adding your own milestones and tasks

  

**Inside each goal you'll find:**

- A **progress bar** showing how far along you are

- A list of **milestones** (major checkpoints) and **tasks** (individual action items)

- The ability to **mark tasks complete** right from the goal detail screen

- A **stake** — an optional financial commitment that makes you put money on the line (see Wallet section)

  

**Goal categories available:**

Health & Fitness · Career & Work · Finance & Money · Skills & Learning · Relationships · Mindset & Wellbeing · Leisure & Adventure · Home & Environment · Giving & Contribution · Personal · Other

  

---

  

### Habits — Build Your Daily Routines

  

Habits are the small, repeated actions that compound into big change over time. The Habits section is designed to help you build and maintain consistent routines.

  

**When you create a habit you can:**

- Give it a name, color, and icon so it feels personal

- Choose the **type**:

  - *Binary* — did you do it or not? (e.g., "Meditate today")

  - *Quantitative* — track a number toward a target (e.g., "Drink 2 liters of water")

- Set the **frequency**: daily, every few days, weekly, only weekdays, only weekends, or specific days of the week

- Set a **duration**: open-ended, with an end date, or with a target number of completions

- Optionally **stake money** on it — if you miss an occurrence, a small charge is automatically applied to your saved card

  

**Inside each habit:**

- A **heatmap calendar** showing your completion history at a glance

- Your current **streak** and longest streak

- A breakdown of completion rate over time

- An option to **give up** the habit (with a calculated penalty if you have a stake)

  

---

  

### Projects — Manage Complex Multi-Task Work

  

Projects are for bigger initiatives that don't fit neatly into a single goal. Think "Launch my podcast" or "Renovate the bathroom" — things with many moving parts.

  

- Create a project with a title, description, and deadline

- Organize it into **sections and tasks**

- Track overall progress as tasks get completed

  

---

  

### Calendar — See Everything in One Place

  

The Calendar screen gives you a **bird's-eye view** of your schedule.

  

- Browse by month and tap any day to see what was planned

- The **Day Detail view** shows all tasks and habits scheduled for that date

- Completed items are visually distinct from pending ones

- Great for reviewing past days or planning ahead

  

---

  

### Social Hub — Accountability With Real People

  

The Social section is where Skinngit goes beyond a simple to-do app. Here you can connect with other users and use **social accountability** to stay on track.

  

#### Feed

- Browse public **commitments** made by other users in the community

- Filter to see only people **you follow**

- React, encourage, and keep an eye on others' progress

  

#### Commitment Board

A commitment is a public declaration: *"I will do X by Y date."* You post it publicly so others can see and verify it.

  

- Create a commitment with a title, deadline, and optional **shame duration** (if you fail, a shame badge stays on your profile for that many days)

- Set visibility: **Private**, visible to **Followers**, or visible to **Accountability Partners**

- **Resolve** your own commitment as completed or failed when the time comes

- Other users can **verify** your completion or **call you out** if they think you failed

  

#### Partners — Your Accountability Circle

Partners are a higher level of connection than followers. A partner relationship is **mutual and approved by both sides**.

  

- Send a **partner request** to someone whose goals align with yours

- Once accepted, you appear in each other's partner list

- Partners can see each other's partner-visibility content

- You can find potential partners through the **Matchmaking** board — post that you're looking for an accountability partner for a specific area (fitness, learning, etc.)

- When a goal with a stake is forfeited, the staked amount can be distributed to your partners as a reward for holding you accountable

  

#### Following

- **Follow** any public user to see their activity in your feed

- View someone's public profile to see their active goals and public commitments

- Browse **Followers** and **Following** lists, plus a dedicated **Partners** tab

  

---

  

### Wallet — Financial Stakes & Payment Cards

  

The Wallet is where Skinngit gets serious. **Financial stakes** are one of the most powerful tools for accountability — loss aversion is a real psychological force, and putting money on the line dramatically increases follow-through.

  

#### How Stakes Work

When you create a goal or habit, you can attach a **financial stake**:

1. Save a debit or credit card in the Wallet

2. Set a stake amount (in CZK) on a specific goal or habit

3. If you **succeed** — your card is never charged

4. If you **fail** — the staked amount is automatically charged to your saved card

5. The money goes to a **charity of your choice** (UNICEF, WHO, Red Cross, Doctors Without Borders, and others) or to your **accountability partners**

  

This isn't a punishment — it's a **pre-commitment device**. You decide upfront what failure costs, which makes quitting much harder.

  

#### Payment Cards

- Add and manage your saved cards securely (powered by **Stripe**)

- Set a default card for automatic charges

- View which goals and habits currently have active stakes

  

#### Transaction History

- Full log of all charges and payments

- See exactly which goal or habit triggered each transaction

  

---

  

### XP, Coins & Levels — Make Progress Feel Good

  

Skinngit tracks your effort with a **gamification system** that rewards consistency.

  

- **XP (Experience Points)** — earned by completing tasks, keeping habits, fulfilling commitments, and other actions

- **Coins** — a secondary currency earned alongside XP

- **Level** — your overall rank, which grows as you accumulate XP

- A **stats banner** on the home screen shows your current XP, level, and coins at a glance

- Special XP bonuses for social actions like keeping a public commitment

  

---

  

### Profile — Your Personal Space

  

Your profile is your identity inside Skinngit.

  

- Upload a **profile photo**

- Set your **username** and **bio**

- View your **follower count**, **following count**, and **partner count**

- See your **public goals and commitments** as others see them

  

#### Settings

- Edit your profile details

- Manage **notification preferences** (which alerts you receive and when)

- Access the **About** page for app information

  

---

  

## Privacy & Visibility

  

You are always in control of who sees what. Every goal, habit, and commitment has a visibility setting:

  

| Setting | Who can see it |

|---|---|

| **Private** | Only you |

| **Followers** | You + anyone who follows you |

| **Partners** | You + your approved accountability partners |

## Getting Started — A Suggested Path

  

1. **Sign up** and complete the short onboarding

2. **Create your first goal** — pick something meaningful, set a deadline, and let the AI generate a plan

3. **Add one habit** — something small and daily that supports your goal

4. **Save a card** in the Wallet and add a small stake to your goal (even a tiny amount works — it's about the commitment, not the money)

5. **Find one person to follow** or post a matchmaking request to find an accountability partner

6. **Check the Home screen every morning** and tick off your tasks and habits one by one

## Frequently Asked Questions

  

**Is my money safe?**

Yes. Card payments are handled by Stripe, one of the world's most trusted payment processors. Skinngit never stores your card details — only a secure token.

  

**What happens if I fail a goal with a stake?**

The staked amount is charged to your saved card and donated to the charity you chose when setting up the stake. You decide the destination before you start.

  

**Can I remove a stake after I set it?**

Stakes are intentionally hard to remove — that's the point. The friction is a feature.

  

**Can I delete a habit?**

You can delete a habit only on the same day you created it. After that, it becomes a commitment you've made to yourself.

  

**Is the social feed anonymous?**

No — commitments and goals shared publicly are tied to your username. Accountability only works when it's real.

  

**What languages does the app support?**

The app interface is in English.

## Brand Story

Most people have goals. Few people finish them. The difference isn't talent or motivation — it's whether you have something real to lose.

"Skin in the game" is a principle used by investors, warriors, and leaders for centuries. Nassim Taleb wrote a whole book about it. The idea is simple: people make better decisions and work harder when they personally bear the consequences of failure.

Skinngit applies this principle to personal goals. Not with streaks. Not with badges. Not with motivational quotes. With real money on the line.

The name says it all — **Skin** (skin in the game) + **n** (and) + **git** (get it done). Three ideas fused into one word. For developers, it carries an extra layer: a Git commit is permanent and irreversible. So is your stake.

**Skinngit isn't your cheerleader. It's your contract.**

It is a silent witness to your commitment — watching your deadline, holding your stake, waiting for the outcome. No emotions. No excuses. No exceptions.

_Fair without mercy._

## Why It Works — Behavioral Science

Loss aversion is one of the most robust findings in behavioral economics (Kahneman & Tversky). People are approximately twice as motivated by potential loss than potential gain. Skinngit doesn't fight human psychology — it uses it.

- **2x** — people work twice as hard to avoid losing $50 than to gain $50
- **92%** — of self-set goals fail without external accountability
- **0** — amount charged if you complete your goal
