# Skinngit — User Guide

> Everything you can do in the app, explained simply for everyday users.

---

## What Is Skinngit?

Skinngit is a personal growth app built around one core idea: **accountability works better when something is actually on the line.** You can track goals, habits, projects, and one-off tasks — and optionally put real money behind them so you actually follow through. The app combines financial stakes, social accountability, and a progression system (XP, achievements, skin score) to keep you moving forward.

---

## Navigation — The Bottom Bar

The app has six main sections accessible from the bottom bar:

| Tab | What it is for |
|-----|----------------|
| **Home** | Your daily dashboard — everything due today |
| **Goals** | Long-term goals broken into milestones and tasks |
| **Projects** | Structured work with timelines and brainstorming |
| **Tasks** | One-off to-dos that do not belong to a goal or project |
| **Habits** | Daily or recurring routines you want to build |
| **Profile** | Your stats, achievements, settings, and subscription |

Calendar, Wallet, and Social Hub are reachable from within the app (via buttons and banners) but not pinned to the tab bar.

## Home Screen

The Home screen is your command center for the day. Every time you open the app it shows everything that needs attention right now.

### What you see on Home

**Greeting and date strip**
A scrollable week strip lets you tap any day to jump to that day in the Calendar. Today is always highlighted.

**Social Hub banner**
A quick-access card linking to the Social section. If someone sent you a partner request, a badge shows the number of pending requests.

**Today's Missions (progress bar)**
Shows how many of today's tasks and habits you have completed vs. the total due today. Updates in real time as you check things off.

**Stakes banner**
If you have active financial stakes on goals, habits, or tasks, a yellow banner shows how much money is on the line right now. This is intentional — it is meant to motivate you to complete your work.

**Habits strip**
A horizontal row of your habits due today. Tap a habit card to mark it done or undo it. Tap "See all" to open the full Habits screen.

**Goals strip**
A horizontal row of your active goals, each showing its progress percentage. Tap any goal to open it. Tap "See all" to go to the Goals screen.

**Projects strip**
Pill-shaped buttons for each active project. Tap one to open it.

**Today's task groups**
Your tasks are grouped by where they come from:
- Goal tasks are grouped under their goal name and can be collapsed or expanded.
- Standalone tasks (from the Tasks tab) appear under "My Tasks."
- Project tasks appear under their project name.

Tap the circle next to any task to mark it complete. Tap the task card itself to expand its description. Goal tasks have an arrow to navigate directly to the goal.

**Coming Up**
A preview of the next 5 upcoming tasks from your goals, with a label showing how many days away each one is.

**"Give Life Your Plan" button**
When you have nothing scheduled today, a button appears. Tapping it opens a quick-create menu so you can add a Task, Goal, Project, or Habit.

## Goals

Goals are the big things you want to achieve — "Run a marathon," "Launch my business," "Learn Spanish." They have a deadline, a category, and are broken into milestones and tasks.

### Creating a Goal

Tap the **+** button on the Goals screen. You fill in:

- **Title** — what you want to achieve
- **Description** — optional, but the more detail you give the AI, the better the plan it builds for you
- **Category** — choose from Health & Fitness, Career, Finance, Skills, Relationships, Mindset, Leisure, Home, Giving, Personal, or Other
- **Start date and target date** — when you begin and when you aim to finish
- **Experience level** — Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced (adjusts how the AI sets the difficulty of the plan)

**Optional: Public commitment**
Toggle this on to post your goal to the public board. Other users can see it and follow your progress.

**Optional: Accountability partner**
Search for another user by their @username. You choose the type of accountability:
- *Verify only* — your partner confirms when you finish.
- *Partner sees my fail* — if you miss, your partner gets a notification about it.
- *Custom consequence* — you write your own penalty in text (e.g. "I buy them coffee").

**Optional: Financial stake**
Put real money on the line. Enter an amount in CZK (minimum 15 CZK, maximum 50,000 CZK). If you do not complete the goal by its deadline, that amount is automatically charged to your saved payment card. You choose where forfeited money goes: App Support, a charity (UNICEF, WHO, Doctors Without Borders, etc.), or your accountability partners.

### AI Plan Generation

After filling in the form, tap **"Generate AI Plan."** The AI analyzes your goal and automatically builds a milestone roadmap with tasks, due dates, and motivational notes. You can:
- Tap any milestone to expand and review its tasks
- Tap "Regenerate Plan" to get a different version
- Save the plan — all milestones and tasks are created automatically

Or tap **"Create Without AI Plan"** to set up milestones manually.

### Goal statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Active | Currently in progress |
| Completed | All milestones finished |
| Failed | Missed the deadline |
| Paused | Temporarily on hold |

Filter goals by status using the chips at the top of the Goals screen.

### Inside a goal

The Goal Detail screen shows overall progress (milestones completed vs. total), each milestone with its tasks, any financial stake attached, and options to edit or mark the goal.

### Milestones

Milestones are checkpoints on the way to your goal. Each has a due date and contains tasks. When all tasks in a milestone are done, the milestone is complete. Goal progress is calculated from milestones.

### Tasks inside goals

Tasks are the daily actions — small enough to do in one sitting. They have a due date so they show up on Home on the day they are due. Tap the dot on the timeline to toggle complete or undo.

## Projects

Projects are for structured, multi-part work — "Build a website," "Plan a trip," "Write a book." Unlike goals, projects have a brainstorming phase before you lock in a plan.

### Two phases

**Brainstorming** (shown with a lightbulb icon)
Free-form ideation. Nothing is committed yet. Use the Brainstorm Canvas to dump ideas, identify problems, and explore solutions before you decide on a direction.

**Locked In** (shown with a lock icon)
You have committed to a plan. Milestones are set, tasks are tracked, and the project appears in your Home screen project strip.

### Creating a Project (4 steps)

1. **Name it** — title and optionally a "Why does this matter?" note (this reappears as a motivational reminder if you have not touched the project in 3+ days)
2. **Category** — for organisation
3. **Target date** — when it should be done
4. **Milestones** — add the major phases and reorder them up or down as needed

### Inside a project

- **Milestone list** — each milestone tracks its own task progress
- **Daily Pulse** — a short daily check-in to log what you worked on and what is blocking you
- **Weekly Review** — a broader weekly reflection on progress
- **Brainstorm Canvas** — a free-form idea board attached to the project
- **Edit project** — update title, dates, category, and motivation text

### Foggy milestone indicator

If you have not added a task to a milestone in over 7 days, it gets flagged visually. This is a gentle nudge that this part of the project needs attention.

## Tasks (Standalone Tasks)

Standalone tasks are simple one-off to-dos that do not belong to any goal or project. Think of them as your personal to-do list — but with teeth if you add a stake.

### Creating a Task (2 steps)

**Step 1 — Details**
- Title and optional description
- Category (optional)
- Due date and optional due time

**Step 2 — Optional stake**
Put money behind it. Enter an amount, pick your saved card, and choose where the forfeit goes (App Support, charity, or partners). If you do not complete the task by the deadline, you are charged automatically.

### My Tasks screen

Shows all your standalone tasks in two tabs:
- **Active** — pending tasks; overdue ones are highlighted
- **History** — completed and failed tasks

The Tasks tab in the bottom bar shows a badge with the number of tasks due today or overdue.

### Completing a task

Tap the task from Home or My Tasks. Mark it complete on the detail screen. If it has a stake, completing it cancels the charge.

## Habits

Habits are routines you want to do repeatedly — daily, a few times a week, or on a custom schedule. The app tracks your streaks and charges you automatically if you miss a day (only if you set a stake).

### Creating a Habit (4 steps)

**Step 1 — Identity**
- Name your habit
- Add an optional context note, e.g. "after my morning coffee" (the "After X" cue)
- Pick an emoji and a colour
- Choose a category

**Step 2 — Schedule**

How often:
| Option | When it applies |
|--------|----------------|
| Every day | Once per day, no exceptions |
| Every 2–6 days | Every other day, every 3 days, etc. |
| Weekdays | Monday through Friday |
| Weekends | Saturday and Sunday only |
| Weekly | Once per week, same day as start date |
| Custom days | Pick specific days of the week |

Duration options:
- Open-ended (no end date)
- Fixed end date
- Target number of completions (e.g. "30 times")

Habit type:
- *Binary* — done or not done (e.g. "Did I meditate today?")
- *Quantitative* — track a number toward a target with a unit (e.g. "30 minutes," "5 km," "10 pages," "8 glasses")

**Step 3 — Stake (optional)**
Set a per-miss charge in CZK. If you miss a scheduled occurrence, that amount is charged to your card that night. You also see the calculated "give up penalty" — what it would cost if you abandon the habit entirely before it ends (10% of remaining occurrences x stake).

**Step 4 — Forfeit destination**
Where the money goes when you miss: App Support, a charity you select, or your accountability partners.

### Logging habits

On the Habits screen, each habit due today appears as a card. Tap the check button on the card to log completion. Tap the card body to open the detail screen.

On Home, the Habits strip shows quick cards — tap any card to toggle done/undone without leaving the screen.

### Habit Detail screen

- **Completion heatmap** — a colour-coded calendar of your full history
- **Current streak** and all-time best streak
- **Completion rate** over time
- Stake information and charges history
- Option to **edit** the habit
- **Give Up** — if you decide to stop the habit, you are charged the give-up penalty if a stake is set. Deleting the habit outright is only allowed on the day you created it.

### Habit Stacks

A habit stack links multiple habits in a sequence using the psychology of "After X, I will Y." Attaching a new habit to an existing one makes it much easier to stick to.

**Creating a stack:**
Tap the chain-link button on the Habits screen header. Give the stack a name and an emoji, then pick habits from your list and arrange them in order.

**Stack Detail screen** shows the full chain formula (e.g. "After Meditate > I will Journal > I will Read"), each habit's status, and lets you reorder or remove habits.

The Calendar shows a full monthly view. Days that have tasks are marked. Tap any day to open the **Day Detail screen**, which lists all goal tasks, standalone tasks, and habits scheduled for that date, along with their completion status.

## Social Hub

Access it via the banner on Home or from the Social option in your Profile.

The Social Hub is your accountability community. It has four tabs.

### Feed tab

A stream of public commitments from other users.
- Filter between **All** (everyone) or **Following** (only people you follow)
- Tap the heart icon to like a commitment and show support

### Board tab

Three views of social commitments:

- **Mine** — your own public commitments. You can mark them resolved (complete or failed) and see partner verifications.
- **Watching** — commitments from people you follow. You can verify them as complete or call them out as failed. A called-out failure adds a shame badge to their profile for the set duration.
- **Public** — open discovery of all public commitments

You can create a new commitment from any of these views. A commitment is a public declaration: "I will do X by Y date." You set a title, link it to an existing goal, habit, or project, set a deadline, and choose visibility (Followers or Partners).

### Partners tab

Your accountability partners — a higher-trust tier than followers.
- Pending partner requests show here; you can Accept or Reject them.
- You can remove existing partners.
- **Partner Matchmaking** — post that you are looking for an accountability buddy. Set your city, interests, and a short message. Browse others doing the same and send them a partner request directly.

### Ranks tab

A community leaderboard. Switch between:
- **XP** ranking — who has the most experience points
- **Streak** ranking — who has the longest consecutive active days
- **Goals completed** ranking

### Following vs. Partners

|  | Followers / Following | Partners |
|--|--|--|
| What they see | Your public commitments | Your goal completions and fails |
| Requires approval | No — one-way | Yes — both sides approve |
| Can share forfeited stakes | No | Yes |

### User Search

Tap the search icon inside Social Hub to find users by username or full name. Debounced search shows results as you type. Tap any result to open their public profile where you can follow them or send a partner request.

### Public Profiles

Every user has a public profile showing their username, level, title, follower/following/partner counts, active public commitments, and their Success and Shame boards.

## Wallet

The Wallet is accessed from your Profile screen or via deep links from goals and tasks.

### Payment Methods

Add a credit or debit card to enable financial stakes. You can save multiple cards and set one as the default. Card details are handled securely by Stripe — the app stores only a secure reference token, never the actual card number.

**To add a card:** Profile > Wallet > Manage > Add Card. Enter your card number, expiry, and CVV, then confirm.

### Stakes at Risk

If you have active stakes on any goal, habit, or task, the Wallet shows a summary of the total amount currently at risk across all of them.

### How charges work

| Scenario | What happens |
|----------|-------------|
| You complete the commitment | No charge, stake is cancelled |
| You miss a goal deadline | Staked amount is charged automatically |
| You miss a habit occurrence | Per-miss amount is charged that night |
| You miss a task deadline | Staked amount is charged automatically |
| You give up a habit early | Give-up penalty is charged immediately |

## Profile

Your profile is your personal record — stats, achievements, progression, and account settings.

### XP and Levels

You earn XP for actions throughout the app:
- Completing goal tasks
- Logging habit completions
- Completing standalone tasks
- Daily logins
- Keeping social commitments
- Maintaining streaks

XP accumulates into levels (shown as "Lv X" on your avatar). Each level has a title that reflects your progression — from early titles like "Rising Star" up through committed achiever tiers.

### Skin Score

The Skin Score is a performance rating based on how consistently you follow through on commitments. It considers your completion rate, streak length, and how often you miss staked commitments.

**Redemption Mode:** If your skin score drops (too many failed commitments), you enter Redemption Mode. A banner appears on Home and Profile reminding you to get back on track. Completing tasks and habits earns your score back up.

### Achievements

Achievements are badges you earn by hitting specific milestones across the app. They are available to all users (free and Premium) and grant bonus XP when unlocked.

The Achievements screen shows your total count (e.g. "12 / 38 Unlocked") and total XP earned from achievements. Badges you have already earned appear first; still-locked ones show what you need to do to unlock them.

You can filter achievements by category:

| Category | What it covers |
|----------|---------------|
| First | First-time actions — first goal, first habit, first stake, first partner |
| Streaks | Streak milestones — 7 days, 30 days, 60 days, and beyond |
| Score | Skin Score milestones — reaching specific score thresholds |
| Goals | Goal completions — first goal, 5 goals, 10 goals completed |
| Habits | Habit consistency — total completions and active habit counts |
| Projects | Project milestones — first project completed, multiple projects |
| Tasks | Task completions — standalone task counts and completion rates |
| Social | Community actions — first follower, first partner, commitment verified |
| Comeback | Redemption — exiting Redemption Mode, recovering your Skin Score |

Achievements are earned automatically — the app checks your progress and awards badges as soon as conditions are met.

### Partner Rewards

Partner Rewards are real-world perks from partner brands — discounts, free trials, vouchers, and credits — available exclusively to **Premium** users who maintain a strong track record.

**How unlocking works:**
Each reward has a specific condition (e.g. "Complete 10 goals," "Maintain a 30-day streak," "Log 100 habit completions"). The app checks these conditions automatically when you open the Rewards screen and unlocks any rewards you have earned.

**Reward categories:**
- Fitness — gym memberships, sports gear discounts, fitness apps
- Education — online courses, learning platforms, book subscriptions
- Productivity — tools, apps, workspace perks
- Wellness — meditation apps, supplements, wellbeing services
- Finance — budgeting tools, investment platforms
- Food — healthy food delivery, meal prep services

**Reward types:** Discounts, Vouchers, Free Trials, Credits

**How to claim:**
Unlocked rewards appear at the top of the Rewards screen with a "Claim" button. Rewards you have already claimed are marked as claimed. In-progress rewards show a progress bar so you can see how close you are.

### Advanced Stats (Premium)

Advanced Stats is a deep analytics dashboard available to Premium users. Access it from the Achievements banner on your Profile screen.

What it shows:

**Overview**
- Total XP earned and current level
- Current streak and overall completion rate across all activity

**Goals**
- Number of active and completed goals
- Number of failed goals
- Goal success rate (completed vs. completed+failed as a percentage)

**Habits**
- Number of active habits
- Number of habits given up

**Tasks**
- Total standalone tasks completed and failed
- Task completion rate percentage

**XP History**
- A log of your last 12 XP-earning events — showing what action earned XP, how much, and when

This gives you a clear picture of where you are strong and where you are losing ground, so you can adjust your approach.

### Google Calendar Sync (Premium)

Google Calendar Sync is a Premium feature that pushes your Skinngit commitments into your Google Calendar so they appear alongside your existing schedule.

**What gets synced:**
- Active goals (due dates appear as calendar events)
- Pending tasks with due dates

**How to connect:**
1. Go to Profile > Settings
2. Tap "Google Calendar Sync"
3. Sign in with your Google account and grant calendar access
4. Your commitments sync automatically

**How to disconnect:**
Go to the same settings page and tap Disconnect. This revokes Skinngit's calendar access and removes the sync. Your existing calendar events are not deleted.

Once connected, any time you create or update a goal or task with a due date, it is reflected in your Google Calendar.

### Your Stats

The Profile screen shows:
- **Followers / Following / Partners** counts — tap any to see the full list
- **Performance stats** — goals completed, habits logged, tasks done, current streak, total XP
- **Success Board** — your completed public commitments
- **Shame Board** — failed public commitments (visible to others for the duration you set when creating them)

### Account and Settings

From Profile you can access:
- **Edit Profile** — update name, photo, city, date of birth, and interests
- **Settings** — general app preferences
- **Notification Settings** — control exactly which push notifications you receive
- **Achievements** — full achievements gallery
- **Rewards** — partner rewards and how to claim them
- **Manage Subscription** — see and manage your current plan
- **Upgrade to Premium** — see what Premium unlocks (unlimited goals, unlimited projects, and more)
- **About** — app information and legal
- **Sign Out**

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Skinngit

**Start small.** Create one goal and one habit. Build the habit of using the app daily before adding more complexity.

**Use the AI plan generator.** It saves significant time and creates a realistic timeline based on your description and experience level. You can always edit the generated plan before saving.

**Set a small stake to begin.** Even 50–100 CZK changes how seriously you take a deadline. You do not need to stake large amounts — loss aversion works at any scale.

**Check Home every morning.** It shows exactly what you need to do today. Treat it like a morning brief.

**Write a strong "Why" on projects.** The app shows this to you as a reminder when you have not touched a project in a few days. A compelling reason is the best motivator.

**Use habit stacks for new routines.** Attaching a new habit to an existing daily action dramatically improves sticking to it. Create a stack and put the new habit right after something you already do automatically.

**Use the Brainstorm Canvas before committing to a project.** Dump all your ideas there first while in brainstorming mode. Only move to "Locked In" when you are confident in the direction.

**Connect with one accountability partner.** Having a real person who can verify or call out your commitments significantly increases follow-through. Use the Matchmaking board to find someone with similar goals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my money safe?**
Yes. Card payments are handled by Stripe, one of the world's most trusted payment processors. Skinngit never stores your card number — only a secure reference.

**What happens if I fail a goal with a stake?**
The staked amount is charged to your saved card and sent to the destination you chose (charity, app support, or partners) when you set up the stake.

**Can I remove a stake after setting it?**
Stakes are intentionally difficult to remove — that friction is the point. The commitment is meant to feel real.

**Can I delete a habit?**
You can delete a habit only on the same day you created it. After that, you can give it up (with a penalty if staked) but not delete it outright.

**Can I undo completing a task?**
Yes. Tap the completion circle again to toggle it back to pending (works for goal tasks and project tasks).

**Is the social feed tied to my real name?**
No — it is tied to your @username, which you set. You control how identifiable you are.

**What is the difference between a goal and a project?**
A goal is outcome-focused (you want to achieve a specific result) and works well with AI-generated milestones. A project is execution-focused (a body of work to complete) and comes with brainstorming tools, daily pulse check-ins, and weekly reviews.

**What is the difference between a task and a goal task?**
A standalone task (in the Tasks tab) is a simple one-off to-do. A goal task lives inside a goal's milestone and is part of a larger plan. Both show up on Home when they are due.

**What happens to my existing goals and habits if I downgrade from Premium to free?**
Your existing data is not deleted. However, if you are over the free plan limits (e.g. you have 5 goals but the free limit is 2), you will not be able to create new items until you are back within the limits. Existing items remain accessible.

**Do I need Premium to use financial stakes?**
No. Financial stakes on goals, habits, and tasks are available on the free plan. Premium is about limits and advanced features, not access to the core accountability tools.
