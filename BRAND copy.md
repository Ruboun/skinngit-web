# instrait — Brand Story & Philosophy

## The Architecture of Voluntary Constraint

### 1. The Universal Escape Hatch

Modern productivity design is built on a fundamental lie: the myth of sustainable motivation. We are told that with the right dashboard, a gamified habit tracker, or enough positive reinforcement, we will naturally evolve into hyper-optimized versions of ourselves.

This ignores the biological reality of human nature.

The human brain is an ancient piece of bio-software, ruthlessly optimized for a single objective: **energy conservation**. When faced with friction, the subcortical brain immediately searches for an escape hatch. It does not matter what the friction is — the biological response is identical:

- The alarm going off at 5:30 AM for a grueling workout.
- A complex, unstructured block of work that requires deep focus.
- A meticulous daily ritual that demands absolute consistency before facing the world.

When a system gives you the freedom to negotiate, you will negotiate. If it allows you to move the deadline, you will move it. Infinite freedom does not breed progress — it breeds high entropy, decision paralysis, and eventual stagnation.

True execution is not born from a sudden spark of inspiration. **True execution is the byproduct of structural limitations.**

---

### 2. The Mechanics of the Strait

In geography, a **strait** is a narrow, compressed passage of water connecting two massive bodies. It is a place of extreme pressure and violent currents. A strait does not create new water — it simply forces existing volume through an unyielding, restricted space. By narrowing the path, it exponentially increases the velocity.

This is the foundational thesis of **instrait**.

It is a deliberate transition from an open, low-pressure ocean of *"I might do this today"* into a high-velocity, single-track corridor of *"This must be done."*

When you enter the strait, you are intentionally shrinking your universe down to a binary outcome. You are removing choice from the equation. You are acknowledging that your future self — the one who will wake up tired, distracted, or unmotivated — cannot be trusted with autonomy.

> **instrait is the tool you use in a moment of absolute clarity to dominate your inevitable moments of weakness.**

**Shrink the freedom. Scale the output.**

---

### 3. The Financial Anchor

An abstract commitment has zero weight. The brain easily dismisses psychological debt because there is no immediate biological feedback loop. To force the bio-software to comply, you must introduce asymmetrical risk. You must translate a digital commitment into tangible, material reality.

By anchoring your objective to a **financial pledge**, instrait aligns your evolutionary survival mechanisms with your goals.

Losing capital hurts. It registers in the brain as a direct threat to resources. When your money is locked behind a pledge, the psychological game changes entirely. Procrastination is no longer free. It suddenly has a clear, non-negotiable price tag.

You are no longer fighting for a vague, distant vision of future success. You are fighting right now to protect your assets. The system uses your own risk aversion as the primary engine of momentum.

---

### 4. The Strait Score

Every time you enter the strait and execute, the system registers it. Every time you break the agreement, the system registers that too.

**Strait Score** is your reputation — not a vanity metric, but a precise reflection of how reliably you follow through on your own word. It is the single number that separates those who plan from those who execute.

The scoring system is honest and unforgiving:
- Completing a pledged commitment raises your score.
- Failing one lowers it.
- Sustained execution unlocks titles: from *Beginner* to *Unbreakable*.
- Consistent collapse triggers **Redemption Mode** — a 7-day streak required to recover your standing.

There are no shortcuts. The score is a mirror.

## The Product — What instrait Does

instrait is a multi-platform execution system (iOS, Android, Web). Every feature is designed around a single loop: **define → pledge → execute → account for the result**.

---

### Goals

A Goal is the highest-level commitment in instrait. It is a defined outcome with a deadline and optional milestones. Goals are not aspirations — they are contracts.

- Break a goal into **Milestones** (checkpoints with their own deadlines) and **Tasks** (discrete actions required to reach each milestone)
- Attach a **financial pledge** to the goal, to individual milestones, or to individual tasks
- Set **visibility**: private, followers only, or accountability partners only
- Goals can be **draft** (private planning state) or active
- On failure, pledged capital is automatically charged via Stripe and routed to a chosen charity or split among accountability partners
- Free plan: 2 active goals maximum. instrait Premium: unlimited

---

### Projects

A Project is a structured, multi-step initiative — closer to a system than a single outcome. Where a Goal defines what you want to achieve, a Project defines how you build something over time.

- Organize work into **phases** and **tasks** with deadlines
- Includes a **Brainstorm Canvas** — a freeform space to capture ideas before structuring them
- Projects are tracked independently from Goals and appear on the dashboard alongside active tasks
- Free plan: 1 project maximum. instrait Premium: unlimited

---

### Habits

A Habit is a recurring commitment enforced on a schedule. It is not a suggestion — every missed occurrence has a financial consequence.

- **Binary habits**: done or not done
- **Quantitative habits**: numeric target with a unit (e.g. 10 pages, 5 km, 8 glasses)
- **Frequency options**: daily, every N days (2–6), weekly, weekdays, weekends, custom days
- **Duration**: open-ended, fixed end date, or target number of completions
- Every missed occurrence triggers a **charge** to the saved payment card at the pledged rate
- **Give-up penalty**: quitting a habit early costs 10% × remaining occurrences × pledge amount
- **Habit Stacks**: chain habits in sequence using the "After [X], I will [Y]" formula — building on existing neural pathways to install new behavior
- Free plan: 5 habits maximum. instrait Premium: unlimited, including stacks

---

### Tasks

A Task is a standalone, one-off commitment with a deadline. It is not attached to a goal or project — it is a direct, pledged action.

- Set a **due date** and optional **due time**
- Attach a financial pledge: fail to complete it by the deadline, and the pledge is charged automatically
- **Time slots**: assign tasks to Morning, Afternoon, or Evening — tightening the perimeter further
- Tasks appear in the **Today's Missions** dashboard strip alongside goal tasks
- Free plan: unlimited tasks (no pledge limit)

---

### Financial Pledges

The pledge system is the enforcement layer that transforms digital commitments into binding agreements.

- Payment cards are saved securely via **Stripe** — no card data is stored on instrait servers
- Pledge amounts are set by the user at commitment creation
- Failed commitments trigger automatic **Stripe Payment Intent** charges
- Charge failures are retried up to 3 times over 24-hour intervals
- All pledge destinations are **predefined charities** (UNICEF, WHO, MSF, and others) or **accountability partners** (for goals set to partner visibility)
- Mobile subscriptions are managed via **RevenueCat** (App Store / Google Play)
- Web subscriptions are managed directly via **Stripe**

---

### Strait Score

Strait Score is the reputation system. It is a number between 0 and 1000 that reflects how reliably a user follows through on their commitments.

- Every completed pledged commitment increases the score
- Every failure decreases it
- The score unlocks **execution titles** that progress as reliability improves:
  - Beginner → Consistent → Reliable → Disciplined → Unbreakable
- **Specialist titles** are awarded based on where the user executes most: Goal Crusher, Creature of Habit, Project Builder, Task Machine, Accountability Partner
- Falling below a threshold triggers **Redemption Mode** — the score is frozen until the user completes a 7-day execution streak
- The Strait Score is visible on the public profile and in the social leaderboard

---

### XP & Levels

XP (experience points) is earned for every completed commitment — goals, habits, tasks, milestones. Unlike Strait Score, XP never decreases. It represents total output, not reliability.

- XP accumulates into **levels** with a daily cap to prevent gaming
- Displayed alongside Strait Score on the profile
- Visible on the leaderboard alongside Strait Score and streak

---

### Social & Accountability

instrait has a social layer designed for accountability, not entertainment.

- **Follow** other users to see their public commitments
- **Accountability Partners**: a higher-trust tier requiring mutual approval. Partners receive a share of forfeited pledges when a goal fails with destination set to "partners"
- **Public Commitment Board**: commitments made visible to followers or partners — identity pressure as a secondary enforcement mechanism
- **Partner Matchmaking**: browse and post partner-seeking profiles by interest area
- **Leaderboard**: ranked by XP, Strait Score, or streak — public signal of execution consistency
- **Visibility levels**: every commitment can be Private, Followers only, or Partners only

---

### Morning Planner

The Morning Planner is a daily entry ritual — a structured prompt to identify the **One Big Thing** for the day and slot tasks into time blocks before the day begins. It enforces intention-setting as a precondition for execution.

## What instrait Is Not

- **Not a motivational app.** instrait does not inspire — it enforces.
- **Not a gamified to-do list.** The pledge mechanic is not a game; it is a binding agreement with your future self.
- **Not a wellness platform.** There are no streaks for streaks' sake, no dopamine rewards for mediocre effort.
- **Not forgiving by design.** Extensions, renegotiations, and soft deadlines are the enemy of execution.

## Color System

Three principles:
1. **Air over layers** — four background levels only. No visual noise, no redundant surfaces. Hierarchy is readable at a glance.
2. **Contrast through space** — readability comes from disciplined use of color, not from quantity. Primary text achieves WCAG AAA contrast. Every color has exactly one role.
3. **Amber = action with consequence** — amber appears in the UI only where a financial commitment is being made or confirmed. One occurrence per screen. Everything else is grey.

### Backgrounds

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Page | `#0D0D12` | Absolute base — entire page |
| Surface | `#13131A` | Primary content area |
| Card | `#1C1C26` | Cards, panels, separated blocks |
| Input / Hover | `#23232F` | Input fields, hover states |

### Text

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#EEEEF2` | Headings, values, key information |
| Secondary | `#9090A0` | Labels, supporting data |
| Muted | `#46464F` | Placeholders, disabled elements, metadata |

### Amber — Primary Accent

Used **exclusively** for CTAs involving a financial pledge, and for displaying active pledge values. One amber element per screen.

| Stop | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Deep | `#180F00` | Warning/pledge box backgrounds |
| Dark | `#6B4200` | Amber element borders |
| Primary | `#E8920A` | CTA button, pledge value display |
| Active | `#F5B84C` | Active state, Strait Score number |
| Tint | `#FDE4A0` | Amber badge backgrounds |

### Semantic Colors

| State | Hex | Background | Border |
|---|---|---|---|
| Completed | `#4ADE80` | `#0A1A0D` | `#1A4A22` |
| Failed | `#F87171` | `#1A0A0A` | `#4A1A1A` |
| Redemption Mode | `#A78BFA` | `#130F1C` | `#2E1F4A` |
| System / Sync | `#60A5FA` | `#0A1020` | `#1A2E50` |

Redemption Mode gets purple deliberately — the only color outside amber/grey signals an exceptional state requiring attention.

### Borders

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Default | `#1C1C24` |
| Strong | `#2E2E3C` |
| Amber accent (active pledge) | `#E8920A` |

Amber border is used exclusively on a card or element with an active (in-progress) pledge. One amber border per screen.

### Strait Score — Color Escalation

Score grows, amber intensity grows with it. Unbreakable is the only title that gets a solid amber background.

| Range | Title | Score Color |
|---|---|---|
| 0–200 | Beginner | `#9090A0` — grey |
| 200–400 | Consistent | `#9B6500` — dark amber |
| 400–600 | Reliable | `#D07D00` — mid amber |
| 600–800 | Disciplined | `#F5B84C` — light amber |
| 800–1000 | Unbreakable | `#0D0D12` text on `#E8920A` background |
